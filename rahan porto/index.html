About Rahan

The Mind Behind Fundaux

Rahan is a serial entrepreneur and visionary leader who has dedicated over a decade to transforming how capital flows to underserved founders and communities. His journey started from the ground up — experiencing first-hand the challenges of accessing funding and the systemic gaps that hold back talented individuals.

As the founder and CEO of Fundaux, Rahan has built a platform that bridges the gap between innovative founders and the resources they need to scale. Under his leadership, Fundaux has deployed over $50 million in capital across 120+ companies globally.

Beyond the boardroom, Rahan is a sought-after speaker, mentor, and advocate for inclusive entrepreneurship. He believes that the next generation of world-changing companies will emerge from places that traditional finance has long overlooked.
